# brag-plan — PhishProbe launch video

**What it is:** PhishProbe checks a suspicious link for you — it opens it inside a
throwaway sandbox on Vultr so you never have to. You get 🟢/🟡/🔴 plus a plain
explanation.

**Who it's for:** anyone who gets phishing SMS/email (parcel, bank, delivery scams).

**What sets it apart:** the product's whole job is to open dangerous links — safely.
The sandbox self-destructs after every scan. *Blast radius: zero.*

**Visual hook:** a real-looking phishing SMS → the link gets scanned → big red
PHISHING verdict with the impersonated brand, then the container self-destructs.

**Tone:** polished, clean, confident tech launch. Not jokey — trustworthy.

**Identity (reused from the real app):** canvas `#0b1120`, panel `#111a2e`,
accents safe `#22c55e` / suspicious `#f59e0b` / phishing `#ef4444`, sky `#0ea5e9`,
system sans. Shield 🛡️ mark.

**Format:** 1920×1080, 30fps, ~20s (600 frames). Music at 120 BPM (0.5s/beat);
every scene cut lands on a beat.
